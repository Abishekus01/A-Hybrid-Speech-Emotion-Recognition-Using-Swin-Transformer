Project Purpose

Understanding emotions through speech to create intelligent and adaptive AI systems.

Purpose of the Project

Detecting human emotions has become an important strategy in today’s intelligent and personalized systems. Understanding a person’s emotional state allows systems to adapt their responses to better suit individual needs and preferences.

In this project, we aim to detect a person’s emotions using speech signals alone, enabling emotion-aware AI applications. Speech Emotion Recognition can be applied in several real-world scenarios such as call centers, where calming music or responses can be provided when a user is angry, or smart vehicles, which can adjust driving behavior when the driver shows signs of stress or fear.

Overall, this application has significant potential in areas such as human–computer interaction, customer experience, safety systems, and intelligent automation, benefiting both organizations and end users.
