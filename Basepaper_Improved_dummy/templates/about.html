SASTRA Emotion Tracker

This project is a mini-project developed by Abishek U S (admin) and Vishwanathan J R (contributor) as part of an academic requirement at SASTRA Deemed University.

The system uses Speech Emotion Recognition powered by Swin Transformer models to identify emotions from audio signals.

  • 🤖 Deep Learning (Transformer)
